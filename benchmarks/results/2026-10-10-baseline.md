# Baseline, 2026-10-10

Before the performance work (commit `acc9631`). Setup and commands:
[`2026-10-10-machine.md`](2026-10-10-machine.md).

| ROM | fps (median of 7 x 3000 frames) | instructions, 300 frames |
|-----|----:|----:|
| 240p-suite   | 797 | 5,159,298,761 |
| testpattern  | 926 | 4,641,855,137 |
| airstriker   | 864 | 5,205,632,789 |
| right2repair | 719 | 5,458,748,021 |
| the-spiral   | 656 | 5,806,931,762 |

Where the time went: the scanline renderer took 50-57% of all
instructions (`render_plane` alone ~30%: it recomputed scroll, cell and
pixel for each of the 320 pixels of each plane; the compositor ran
branchy priority loops per pixel). Next came the 68000 core (operand
helpers called out of line, ~30 instructions per ROM fetch), the YM2612
(computing silent channels), the PSG (ticked one step at a time) and the
resampler.
