frames under callgrind. Wall-clock gains are smaller than instruction
gains: what is left is denser work (interpreter dispatch with its
mispredicted indirect branches, the Z80/68000 interleaving, SIMD code
that counts as one instruction for 16 pixels).

## Step by step

Instructions per 300 frames (G = 10^9) after each commit, in order.

| commit | 240p | pattern | air | r2r | spiral |
|--------|----:|----:|----:|----:|----:|
| baseline | 5.159 | 4.642 | 5.206 | 5.459 | 5.807 |
| vdp: render planes a tile row at a time, composite by score | 3.227 | 2.686 | 3.352 | 3.546 | 3.914 |
| m68k: always inline the effective-address helpers | 3.036 | 2.680 | 3.070 | 3.389 | 3.767 |
| core: one-compare fast path for plain cartridge ROM | 2.925 | 2.596 | 2.961 | 3.265 | 3.635 |
| ym2612: skip the work of operators that have faded out | 2.760 | 2.432 | 2.786 | 3.127 | 3.444 |
| psg: run counters from reload to reload | 2.699 | 2.370 | 2.726 | 3.067 | 3.350 |
| core: inline the Z80's sound RAM accesses | 2.699 | 2.370 | 2.726 | 3.019 | 3.307 |
| vdp: priorities with byte arithmetic (SIMD) | 2.452 | 2.121 | 2.487 | 2.773 | 3.066 |
| vdp: name table row once per scroll column | 2.239 | 1.905 | 2.276 | 2.551 | 2.855 |
| m68k: fixed-size decode table | 2.226 | 1.894 | 2.265 | 2.540 | 2.843 |
| psg: finish ultrasonic tone channels in one step | 2.202 | 1.870 | 2.241 | 2.528 | 2.816 |

Not visible above (headless runs do not take snapshots): saving and
loading byte slices in one copy made the frontend's rewind snapshots ~6x
cheaper (500 snapshots of Airstriker: 292 ms -> 47 ms; 300 rewind steps:
142 ms -> 62 ms).

## Tried, and dropped

* Division -> shift for the tile height in `render_plane`: no
  measurable change (instructions +0.2%, fps within noise).
* A work-RAM fast path in the 68000's bus (like the ROM one): +0.8%
  instructions on Airstriker (its VDP polling paid for the extra
  compares), at most -1% elsewhere.
* Computing both resampler channels in one loop for more
  instruction-level parallelism: no change in instructions or fps.
